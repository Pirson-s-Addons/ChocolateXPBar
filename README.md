# Chocolate XP Bar

Turns the World of Warcraft experience bar into a chocolate bar, in every version of the game. Each of the 20 divisions is a portion, on an empty mold. The chocolate is painted by Blizzard: it comes from the game's Valentine's Day chocolate texture.

Open the options with `/cxb` to choose the chocolate of the bar and of your rested bonus: milk, dark, white, ruby or praline.

Reputation and honor bars are left alone.
